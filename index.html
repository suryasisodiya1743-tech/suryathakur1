What is Digital Marketing?




Digital marketing means promoting products or services using internet platforms like Google, Facebook, Instagram and Email.

Types of Digital Marketing




  • Search Engine Optimization (SEO)
  • Social Media Marketing
  • Email Marketing
  • Content Marketing
  • Google Ads (PPC)

Why Digital Marketing is Important?


It helps businesses reach more customers, increase sales, build brand awareness and grow faster in online world.
